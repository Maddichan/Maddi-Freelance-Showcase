## Core Philosophy - Leadership Foundations

> **Core Principle:** A manager’s primary job is to create conditions for people to do their best work. That requires clarity, consistency, empathy, and intentional communication.

### 👂 Listen First
Effective leadership begins with listening.

**Key Practices:**
* Allow people to fully express themselves
* Ask clarifying questions
* Reflect back what you heard
* Validate emotions without rushing to fix
* Slow down your responses

### 🧠 Know Your People Individually
*One size fits one.*

**Learn for each person:**
* **Motivators:** What drives them forward
* **Stressors and Strengths:** What overwhelms them and where they excel
* **Development Needs:** Areas for professional growth
* **Communication Preferences:** How they receive and process feedback
* **Support Requirements:** What they need from leadership to succeed

### 🎯 Leadership Identity
*This is the foundation of people-centered leadership.*

**What an effective manager shows:**
* Emotional intelligence & self-awareness
* Clarity & structure
* Warmth & curiosity
* Fairness
* Accountability & integrity

## ℹ️ General Information

This playbook outlines the core behaviors and mindsets that help managers create an environment where people can do their best work. Every principle in this guide supports psychological safety, clarity, and trust — the foundations of effective, people-first leadership.

Managers who listen deeply, set clear expectations, communicate consistently, uphold healthy boundaries, and lead with integrity create teams that feel supported, understood, and empowered. These practices build a workplace where individuals can grow, collaborate, and perform at their highest level.

## 🤝 Code of Conduct

### 👥 Leadership Behavior Expectations
Managers model the standards they expect from their teams.

| Behavior | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Respectful, clear communication** | Use direct language, active listening, and constructive tone during all team interactions. |
| **Transparency and honesty** | Share context openly, admit when you don't have all the answers, and communicate changes promptly. |
| **Consistent follow-through** | Honor commitments, stick to agreed-upon check-in schedules, and deliver on promises. |
| **Accountability for actions and decisions** | Take ownership of team outcomes and model responsibility rather than shifting blame. |
| **Protecting psychological safety** | Encourage open dialogue, validate questions, and allow room for learning and mistakes. |

### 🚩 Professional Boundaries
Healthy boundaries create trust and fairness across the team.

| Boundary | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **No downward gossip** | Refrain from discussing company politics, peer performance, or sensitive operational decisions with direct reports. |
| **No venting to direct reports** | Keep personal frustrations and high-level stressors between you, your manager, or HR. |
| **No favoritism** | Treat all team members fairly, using uniform criteria for evaluation and task distribution. |
| **No blurred friendships** | Maintain a clear professional boundary while staying warm and approachable. |
| **No inconsistent standards** | Apply performance metrics, rules, and expectations equally across the entire team. |

### ⏰ Attendance & Reliability

| Expectation | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Arrive prepared and on time** | Be ready for scheduled 1:1s, team check-ins, and group syncs with an agenda. |
| **Communicate delays or absences early** | Notify affected team members as soon as possible if schedule adjustments occur. |
| **Honor commitments and deadlines** | Track promises made during check-ins and consistently follow up on time. |

### ⚖️ Conflicts of Interest

| Requirement | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Disclose potential conflicts immediately** | Report personal or financial ties that could impact objective decision-making to HR or leadership. |
| **Avoid situations that compromise objectivity** | Step back from hiring, evaluating, or assigning tasks where personal bias exists. |

## 🛡️ Policies and Procedures

### 📝 Paid Time Off (PTO)

| Item | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **PTO Accrual & Requests** | Submit PTO requests through the standard company system as far in advance as possible. |
| **Approval Timelines & Blackout Periods** | Managers review requests within 48 hours, considering key project deadlines and coverage. |
| **Fairness & Consistency** | Evaluate requests fairly without favoritism while ensuring operational continuity. |

### ⏰ Work Hours & Flexibility

| Item | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Core Working Hours** | Establish primary overlap hours when all team members should be reachable for collaboration. |
| **Availability Expectations** | Keep calendar statuses updated and communicate status changes during working hours. |
| **Remote-Work Norms** | Set target response windows for chat and email, and establish asynchronous updates across global time zones. |

### 📊 Performance & Feedback

| Item | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Frequent, Specific Feedback** | Deliver actionable feedback during weekly 1:1s using the Situation-Behavior-Impact (SBI) framework. |
| **Quarterly Development Conversations** | Hold dedicated quarterly check-ins focused exclusively on long-term career pathing and skill building. |
| **Regular Expectation Reviews** | Review individual OKRs and role responsibilities at the start of each quarter to keep goals aligned. |

### 💬 Communication Standards

| Item | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Clear & Respectful Language** | State intent clearly, focus on objective facts, and maintain a professional, supportive tone across all channels. |
| **Ask Clarifying Questions** | Default to curiosity when requirements are unclear, confirming next steps and expectations in writing. |
| **Maintain Transparency** | Share context, decisions, and project updates openly across public team channels rather than private messages. |

### ⚠️ Escalation & Issue Resolution

| Item | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Early Communication** | Address small roadblocks or interpersonal friction immediately to prevent escalation. |
| **Guided Problem-Solving** | Help direct reports navigate challenges through constructive questioning and active listening. |
| **Formal HR Escalation** | Report cases involving policy violations, harassment, performance action plans, or severe conflicts to People Operations/HR. |

| Topic | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
